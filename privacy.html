RESET Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2, 2026

RESET provides garage and space reset services for Ellis County, Texas and nearby communities. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information submitted through our website quote form, calls, messages, and customer communications.

Information we collect

We may collect your name, phone number, email address, service address or general location, project details, photos or videos you choose to share, appointment details, and messages you send to RESET.

How we use information

We use this information to respond to quote requests, evaluate your reset project, schedule services, provide customer support, send service-related updates, improve our operations, and comply with legal or carrier requirements.

SMS and messaging consent

When you provide your phone number through the website quote form or another approved consent flow, you authorize RESET to contact you about your quote request, appointments, and service updates. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out and HELP for help.

Consent to receive SMS or automated messages is not a condition of purchase. RESET does not sell, rent, or share SMS opt-in data or consent with third parties for their marketing purposes.

Sharing of information

We may share information with trusted service providers only as needed to operate our website, quote form, CRM, scheduling, communications, payment, or customer service systems. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect rights, safety, and security.

Data security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and operational safeguards to protect customer information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so please avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information.

Your choices

You may request that RESET update, correct, or delete your information when legally and operationally appropriate. You may opt out of SMS messaging by replying STOP.
