Is a garage reset the same as junk removal?

No. Junk removal can be part of a reset, but a reset also includes sorting, organization, zones, and a plan for how the garage should work.

When is junk removal enough?

Junk removal may be enough when almost everything needs to leave and you do not need help reorganizing what stays.

When is a garage reset better?

A garage reset is better when you need help deciding, sorting keepsakes, protecting useful items, building zones, or making room to park again.

Can RESET remove items too?

RESET can coordinate removal as part of the reset plan when the project requires items to leave.
