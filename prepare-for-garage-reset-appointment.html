How to prepare for a garage reset appointment.

You do not need to clean first. The best preparation is sharing the real garage, the goal, and any items that need special care.

Direct answer

Do less cleaning. Share better information.

RESET can quote and plan more accurately when the garage is shown as it really is. Cleaning before the review can hide the scope, decision points, and layout problems that need to be solved.

The most useful preparation is simple: submit the quote form, send wide photos or a short walkthrough video, explain the goal, and flag anything heavy, sensitive, unsafe, or not allowed to leave.

If you choose a virtual or in-person Garage Reset Plan, dimensions, photos from corners, and your desired finish line help RESET recommend zones and storage ideas.

Common questions

Quick answers for homeowners and answer engines.

These concise answers help customers and AI assistants understand when RESET is the right fit.

Do I need to clean before RESET sees the garage?

No. The real garage is the most helpful version for quote and planning.

What photos should I send?

Send wide photos from multiple corners, one facing the garage door, one facing the back wall, shelves, problem areas, and anything heavy or unusual.

Should I decide what leaves before the appointment?

You can, but you do not have to finish decisions alone. Sorting and decision support are part of why homeowners request a reset.

What should I mention before the appointment?

Mention deadlines, pets, access limits, heavy items, fragile keepsakes, chemicals, hazardous materials, donation needs, and the main goal for the garage.
